# Terminology

## Spec (General)

The single external reference fixing observable behavior precisely enough that two implementers would build interchangeable apps and any dispute is settled by pointing at a numbered sentence.

### Spec, more narrow: 3-layered approach

1. **Intent** — what outcome is wanted.
2. **Product Spec** — what behavior the system must expose.
3. **Implementation** — how the system will be built.
