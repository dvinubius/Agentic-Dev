# Explain in Simplified Technical English

Explain the subject matter in the spirit of ASD-STE100.

- Keep sentence structures direct.
- Limit procedural steps to single actions.
- Avoid unnecessary synonyms — one term per concept, used consistently.
- Include mermaid diagrams where applicable and helpful.
